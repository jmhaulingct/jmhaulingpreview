Quick answers.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. Curbside and driveway pickups may be completed without you present once the details and payment arrangements are confirmed.

Can you remove items from basements or upstairs?

Yes. Include photos of stairs, hallways and tight access points so the quote reflects the work required.

How quickly can my junk be removed?

Availability depends on the job and schedule. Choose your preferred timeframe in the form and JM Hauling will confirm what is available.

Can every job be quoted from photos?

Many can. Larger or more complex projects may require a free in-person estimate before final pricing.
